Every system begins by drawing a line around something and giving it a name. The line makes the thing easier to hold, but it also hides everything that did not fit inside. A useful map is still a map. A convincing interface is still an argument about what deserves to be seen.

This journal is a place to inspect those arguments. Some entries will be about software and the machines that carry it. Others will be about attention, images, habits, and the peculiar ways ordinary life resists being reduced to a model.

## A working premise

Certainty is often just a story with excellent typography. That does not make stories useless. It means they should be handled with care: tested against experience, revised when they stop explaining the world, and left unfinished when the honest answer remains unfinished.

> The point is not to escape illusion. The point is to notice which illusion is doing the work.

The notes collected here will remain small on purpose. A short observation can keep its edges. It can invite disagreement without first building a fortress around itself.

So this is the first mark in the ledger: pay attention, make the hidden choice visible, and leave enough quiet around an idea for it to change.
